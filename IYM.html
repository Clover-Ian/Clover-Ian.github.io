IYM AutoClicker

A flexible mouse-clicking tool with safety controls and multiple positioning options.

IYM AutoClicker is created by Max Qiu.

Safety comes first.

Safe mode limits fast intervals and can stop automatically under heavy load. The app can also recommend an interval based on your computer.

Click your way.

Choose to follow the pointer, loop through multiple points, or click at a fixed position. Select either a left or right click.

More with Member.

Member features include Text Input, Key Repeat, random intervals, timer start/stop, adjustable random click offset, and a Cyberpunk-style background. Discover AutoClicker Pro.
