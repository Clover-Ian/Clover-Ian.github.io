Privacy Policy

This policy describes how IYS handles information when you visit this website, create an IYS account, contact us, or use IYS offerings other than IYS AutoClicker Pro. The AutoClicker Pro purchase process is covered by its separate Privacy Policy.

Information We Handle

If you create an account, the account service processes your email address, password, and any display name you provide. We use Supabase to provide account authentication. Your sign-in session is saved in your browser so you can remain signed in. If you email us, we receive the contact information and message you choose to send.

When you visit the site, our hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser, and request details to deliver and protect the site. We do not intentionally collect payment card details through these general website pages.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate and secure the website and account features, respond to questions, and maintain or improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Service Providers and External Links

Supabase provides account authentication, and our website hosting provider serves the site. Email providers process messages you send us. These providers handle information under their own privacy terms. Our pages may link to third-party services; their privacy practices are controlled by those services, not IYS.

Retention and Your Choices

We keep account information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You can request account deletion or contact us about your information at iys-ian@outlook.com. Deleting an account may not immediately remove copies retained by service providers or information we must keep for legal or security reasons.

Because your account session is saved in your browser, sign out when using a shared device. You can also clear your browser's site data to remove the locally stored session.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, email iys-ian@outlook.com.
