Questions

Do I need Pro to use IYS Archiver?

Only for the Cyberpunk theme. Twelve output formats, AES-256, split volumes and every extraction are free.

Is this a subscription?

No. You buy one period — a month for $1 or a year for $8 — and nothing renews on its own. The key only unlocks the Cyberpunk theme; every other feature is free.

Why by email instead of a checkout page?

Keys are issued by hand, so there is no card form and no payment provider in the middle. You get a real person on the other end, and we can sort out an unusual setup before you pay.

How do I pay?

WeChat Pay, AliPay or PayPal — say which one in the email and we send the matching link with the reply. No card details are collected on this site.

How long does the key take?

Up to a week, usually much less. One person runs this, so please allow the time.

Can I use the same key on a second Mac?

Yes, as long as you sign in with the same IYS account. The key belongs to the account, not to a machine.

What do I need?

macOS 26 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac, and an IYS account (free) to hold the key.

What if it doesn't work for me?

See the refund policy, or email iys-ian@outlook.com and we'll sort it out.
