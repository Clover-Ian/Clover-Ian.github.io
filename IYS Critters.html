IYS Critters

Adopt a small handmade creature. Feed it, talk to it, keep an eye on it — and one day find out how its story ends.

Not open yet

You'll need an IYS account. Nothing to download.

One, or a few.

Design your own from scratch, or pick one of the ready-made kinds. Every creature is generated from its own seed, so no two look the same. You can keep several at once — they share the same little world.

Two kinds of life.

Some arrive with a deadline: thirty days until they have to go back to their planet. Others stay as long as you keep them alive. Both can be lost.

They do their own thing.

Leave them alone and they'll make their own choices — play games all night, sulk, rearrange your things, forget to eat. You'll only find out when you come back.

A week is a long time.

If you don't visit for more than a week, they don't make it. No countdown warning after the fact — just a note on the door.

How it ends.

When a creature leaves or dies, the site plays its ending: a hand-drawn animated scene on the left, their story scrolling on the right, and a piano piece recorded for the occasion.

Still being drawn.

The creatures are being drawn by hand, the readings are being written, and the piano is still being practised. When it's ready, this page will open the door.
